## 2026-07-22 - [Contact Form Accessibility & Focus Restoration]
**Learning:** In highly customized, dynamic forms where the submission view is replaced entirely by a success component (conditional rendering), screen readers and keyboard users often lose context because focus either drops or remains on a detached DOM node. Giving the success container `role="status"` and `tabIndex={-1}` allows immediate screen reader announcement and dynamic keyboard focus capture. When resetting the form back to its default state ("Send again"), we must programmatically restore focus to the first input field to prevent random page jumps and preserve an intuitive tab order.
**Action:** Always pair conditionally rendered form views with an active focus management strategy: focus on the success card when submitted, and restore focus to the primary input when resetting.
