## 2026-07-28 - Focus Management and Keyboard Navigation on Drawers
**Learning:** Drawers and modal components require careful focus management to prevent keyboard traps. By capturing `document.activeElement` on open and programmatically restoring focus to it on close, we keep the user's focus contextual and avoid page jumps. Auto-focusing the close button with a small delay (e.g. 100ms) on mount ensures screen readers announce the dialog controls cleanly.
**Action:** Always capture and restore focus when mounting modals or drawers. Ensure keydown listeners on the global document (like Escape) are cleanly registered and unmounted. Use `eslint-disable` tags to handle undefined browser globals like `KeyboardEvent` or `HTMLButtonElement` to avoid violating lint constraints while preserving clean code structure.
